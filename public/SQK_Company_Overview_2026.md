# SQK Company Overview 2026

## Positioning
SQK is a Quantum-AI-HPC platform company building validated, secure, and deployable advanced-computing solutions for high-trust industries. The company leads with **QMedic** as its focused commercial product for medical imaging workflows, supported by reusable platform capabilities across validation, orchestration, and physics-informed modeling.

## Core Message
SQK is not presenting five unrelated products. SQK is presenting **one focused commercial wedge, QMedic**, backed by a reusable Quantum-AI-HPC platform that can expand into scientific computing, industrial optimization, hybrid HPC, and secure medical AI.

## Commercial Product Focus
### QMedic
QMedic is SQK’s lead commercial product. It targets CT/MRI-oriented reconstruction, refinement, comparison, and quality governance for high-trust medical imaging workflows. The commercialization path begins with research and validation settings, expands into paid pilots and API integrations, and can evolve toward enterprise deployments as regulatory, clinical, and data-governance pathways mature.

## Platform Foundation Behind QMedic
- **QTAU** validates through benchmarking, reproducibility, quality metrics, scorecards, and reporting.
- **Qukkos** orchestrates hybrid workflows across classical, quantum, and HPC resources.
- **QPINN** models through physics-informed learning and optimization for scientific and industrial workloads.
- **SuperQuantumStation** creates ecosystem access through education, developer touchpoints, and partner visibility.

## Why Now
- Medical AI is moving from experimentation to controlled deployment, increasing the need for quality governance and auditable workflows.
- Quantum and HPC ecosystems are converging through hybrid workloads, increasing demand for benchmarking and orchestration layers.
- Enterprise AI adoption is becoming more security-conscious, expanding the need for secure deployment and PQC-aware roadmaps.
- High-trust industries increasingly prefer measurable reliability, reproducibility, and audit trails over broad performance claims.

## Business Model
- **QMedic:** research SaaS, paid pilots, API access, project deployments, then hospital/enterprise licensing and OEM integration.
- **QTAU:** benchmarking reports, validation service, PoC support, then annual validation subscriptions and audit/report modules.
- **Qukkos:** workflow integration projects, then enterprise orchestration engine and managed workflow delivery.
- **QPINN:** specialized R&D/modeling projects, then optimization licenses and simulation modules.
- **SuperQuantumStation:** subscription, education, and developer access, then ecosystem programs and partner marketplace leverage.

## Go-to-Market Sequence
1. Lead with QMedic in medical imaging.
2. Attach QTAU trust evidence to QMedic.
3. Convert project credibility into paid pilots.
4. Package repeatable modules such as dashboards, APIs, benchmark reports, connectors, and deployment playbooks.
5. Expand into enterprise licensing and recurring support contracts.
6. Use SuperQuantumStation as an ecosystem channel rather than the primary enterprise sales narrative.

## Growth Roadmap
### 0–12 months
Focus on QMedic pilot pipeline, investor website, collateral, and case studies, while packaging QMedic MVP/PoC, QTAU benchmark reports, and integrated demos.

### 12–24 months
Convert pilots into recurring contracts and API usage through QMedic SaaS/API, QTAU enterprise validation, and Qukkos workflow integration.

### 24–36 months
Scale platform revenue globally with cross-solution integration and secure medical AI stack expansion supported by PQC-aware architecture.

## Investor Thesis
SQK is building a validated Quantum-AI-HPC platform for high-trust industries, with **QMedic as the near-term commercial wedge** and **QTAU / Qukkos / QPINN / SuperQuantumStation** as reusable platform capabilities that compound over time.
